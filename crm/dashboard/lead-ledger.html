How the numbers work

Revenue

In-force policies count the advance Axiums expects (75% of annual premium × comp rate). Where Axiums has no advance yet (Ethos, Corebridge), it is estimated from your comp table and marked “est.” Pending and approved policies show as pipeline, not net.

Chargebacks

Taken from Axiums’ per-policy commission actuals and its chargeback list. A lapsed or not-taken policy counts what the carrier paid minus what it clawed back.

Meta batches

Ad receipts are grouped by the week they were billed (Monday start). Leads from MyEZLeads count toward the week they arrived; duplicates and Meta test leads are removed.
